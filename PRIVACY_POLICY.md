## 1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tagmate Analytics
("**we**," "**us**") collects through our inventory management platform (the
"**Service**"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the
staff accounts of the medical spas and clinics ("**Customers**") that use the
Service. It does not apply to a Customer's own patients — see Section 6.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you or your clinic's admin creates an account:
your email address, the name you provide, your clinic's name, and your role
(admin or staff).

**Inventory and business data your clinic enters.** Product names, SKUs,
barcodes, RFID tag IDs, lot numbers, expiration dates, storage locations,
vendor contact details, unit costs, and usage/checkout events (when a product
was marked used, and by implication, roughly how often). This data is about
your clinic's products and operations, not about your patients — see Section
6.

**Usage information.** Basic technical logs generated by using the Service
(e.g. login timestamps, error logs) that help us operate and troubleshoot the
platform.

**Payment information** *(once paid plans launch)*: handled directly by our
payment processor (e.g. Stripe) — we don't store full card numbers ourselves.

We do not currently use tracking cookies or third-party advertising/analytics
scripts in the Service.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

- Provide, maintain, and secure the Service, including keeping one clinic's
  data isolated from another's;
- Authenticate accounts and enforce the roles/permissions your admin sets;
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests;
- Generate the analytics and reorder recommendations the Service is built
  to provide, computed from your own clinic's historical data;
- Send you service-related communications (e.g. security notices, changes to
  these policies) and, if you opt in, product updates;
- Improve the Service, including in aggregated or de-identified form that
  doesn't identify your clinic;
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties
for their own marketing purposes.

## 4. Who We Share Information With

- **Supabase** (our database and authentication provider) — hosts your
  account and inventory data, and enforces the row-level security that
  keeps one clinic's data invisible to another's.
- **Our application hosting provider** (currently Railway/Streamlit
  Community Cloud) — runs the Service's application code.
- **Our payment processor** *(once paid plans launch)* — e.g. Stripe,
  to process subscription payments. They receive your billing details
  directly; we don't see or store your full card number.
- **Law enforcement or regulators**, only if legally required (e.g. a valid
  subpoena), and to the extent required by that legal process.
- **A successor** in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets,
  subject to the same commitments in this policy.

We don't share your data with anyone else without your consent.

## 5. Data Security

We rely on Postgres Row Level Security so that one clinic's login can never
retrieve another clinic's data — this is enforced by the database itself, not
just by application code. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and
passwords are hashed and managed by our authentication provider — we never
see or store a plaintext password.

No system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If we
become aware of a breach affecting your data, we'll notify you as required by
applicable law.

## 6. About Your Patients' Data

**The Service is designed to track products, not patients**, and you should
not enter any patient-identifying information into it (see Section 2 of the
Terms of Service). We don't collect, and don't want to collect, protected
health information about your patients. If information about an individual
patient ever ends up in the Service despite this policy, contact us at
`[Support Email]` so we can help you remove it.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your account and inventory data for as long as your account is
active. If your account is terminated, we retain your data for a limited
period (currently planned as 30 days) to allow for export or reactivation,
after which we may delete it. You can request deletion sooner, or an export
of your data, by contacting `[Support Email]`.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

You can access and update most of your account information directly within
the Service. You can request a copy of your clinic's data, or request
deletion of your account and its data (subject to Section 7 and any
legitimate business or legal need to retain certain records), by contacting
`[Support Email]`.

If you're located somewhere with specific statutory privacy rights (for
example, the EU/UK or California), you may have additional rights — contact
us and we'll do our best to accommodate the request, and we'll expand this
policy with the specific legal disclosures those laws require once we have
customers in those regions.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is a business tool intended for use by adult staff of medical
spas and clinics. It's not directed at, and we don't knowingly collect
information from, children.

## 10. International Data

Our infrastructure providers may process data in the United States (or
wherever they operate their servers). By using the Service, you consent to
this processing.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material
change, we'll notify you (e.g. by email or an in-app notice) before it takes
effect.

## 12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or a request regarding your data?
